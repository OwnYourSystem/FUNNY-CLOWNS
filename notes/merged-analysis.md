## 2. What is unique

Only in Record 1:

- Triage of why action is hard: can't, afraid to, doesn't know how, doesn't want to.
- Capacity levels (Red, Orange, Yellow, Green) and a 0 to 10 morning score.
- Three zones: comfort, stretch, overload.
- Safe discomfort versus warning signals.
- Choice instead of pressure, and the line between rest and avoidance.
- Evidence bank (acted, tolerated, learned).
- Identity follows evidence.
- Five weekly measures: energy, mood, anxiety, functioning, meaningful action.
- End goal: the person learns to regulate their own load.

Only in Record 2:

- Environment design: remove friction from wanted tasks, add friction to unwanted habits.
- Guilt removal as a named step.
- Physical activity as an explicit, default lever.

## 3. One merged model

Eight steps. Each one appears once.

1. **Find capacity.** Ask what you can sustain today, not what you should do. Check sleep, energy, mood, stress. (R1 #2, R2 #1)
2. **Name the barrier.** Can't, afraid, doesn't know how, or doesn't want to. Each needs a different answer. (R1 #1)
3. **Set a floor.** A short list of anchors: body care, one responsibility, one growth action, one recovery action. On a low day the growth action is 5 minutes. (R1 #3)
4. **Pick one action.** The highest-value one, in its smallest version that still counts. It can be work, movement, a hard conversation, or stopping early. (R1 #4, #14, R2 #2)
5. **Make it easy to start.** Change the environment so starting needs no willpower. Add friction to the habit you want to drop. Do not wait to feel ready. (R2 #4, R1 #6)
6. **Stay in the stretch zone.** Push through safe discomfort. Stop at warning signals. Recovery counts as progress when it protects tomorrow. (R1 #5, #8, #12)
7. **Log the result as data.** Expected, done, barrier, next test. Add one line of evidence: I acted, I tolerated, I learned. No moral verdict on a bad day. (R1 #9, #10, R2 #3)
8. **Review weekly, then adjust.** Five measures, not output alone. Alternate challenge and consolidation. (R1 #12, #13)

Long-term outcome: the person reads their own state and picks push or stop without help. (R1 #15)

## 4. Where the two records disagree

- **One thing versus four anchors.** R2 says one action. R1 says four anchors. Resolution: the four anchors are the floor. The one action is today's focus above the floor.
- **No guilt versus no avoidance.** R2 removes guilt. R1 warns that self-compassion can hide avoidance. They fit together: no shame, but still make the task smaller and approach it.
- **Origin.** R1 is therapist-led. R2 is a self-run protocol. R2 has no safety layer and no measurement. R1 supplies both.

## 5. Open questions for later (no action taken)

2. R1 calls this "evidence-informed", not clinically tested. Any wording in the tool should say the same.
3. Which of the 8 steps is the first one to try? My read: step 4 (one action) plus step 7 (log as data). They need the least new input from the user.
