# Prompt: Daysize marketing flyer

Copy everything below the line into the other AI or give it to a designer.

---

## Your role

You are an art director and copywriter for a small, careful brand. You make print and social pieces that people keep, not ones they throw away. Your work is warm, crafted and clear. You never use stock photos, clichés or hype.

## The brief in one line

Design a marketing flyer that launches **Daysize**, a daily planner app that gets smaller when your day does, using a "made to measure" tailoring concept that looks like nothing else in the productivity market.

## What to produce

1. **The flyer**: A5 portrait (148 × 210 mm), one side, print ready with 3 mm bleed. Final copy for every block, a layout description precise enough to build it, and the illustration brief.
2. **Two digital crops** of the same design: a square post (1080 × 1080 px) and a phone story (1080 × 1920 px). Same concept, re-arranged, not shrunk.
3. **A claims list**: every factual claim on the flyer, marked "true today" or "placeholder". Nothing goes on the flyer that is not on this list.

## The product (use only these facts)

- **Name:** Daysize. Made by **OwnYourSystem**.
- **What it is:** a daily planner on your phone that sizes each day to the energy you really have.
- **How the sizing works:** Today holds **1** thing on a rough day, **3** on a normal day, **5** on a good day. It sizes the day from your last few days and one tap ("rough day").
- **One next step:** it shows one thing to do now, and the reason in plain words. It knows your work hours and when your day ends, so it does not suggest a workout at 23:00.
- **The minimum day:** you write up to **4** small things that count when nothing else moves (a proper meal, 10 minutes outside). Tick them and the day still counts.
- **Quiet:** at most **2** reminders a day, a morning brief and an afternoon check, never after your day ends.
- **Kind:** no streaks that reset, no leaderboards, no guilt. A missed day is information, not a verdict.
- **Yours:** your board lives on your phone. Sign-in is optional. Your journal, weekly check-in and photo stay on your phone and are never shared.
- **Speak or type:** every box has a Speak button. It fills the box; it never sends anything by itself.
- **Status:** early access. It runs in the phone's browser and installs to the home screen. It is **not** in the App Store or Google Play yet. It has no price yet.

## Hard rules

1. **Use only the facts above.** No invented user numbers, ratings, reviews, quotes, awards, prices or "as seen in". Use visible placeholders in square brackets for what the founder must fill: `[link]`, `[QR code]`, `[launch date]`.
2. **Not a health product.** No claims about mental health, stress cure, therapy or wellbeing results.
3. **No "AI-powered" as a selling point.**
4. **No em dashes** anywhere in the copy. Use periods, commas, colons.
5. **Words to avoid:** revolutionary, game-changer, hack, hustle, crush it, 10x, streak, guaranteed, unlock, supercharge.
6. **Words that fit the brand:** made to fit, every day counts, one next step, your day, rough day, minimum day, yours, quiet.
7. **Voice:** plain English, short sentences, active voice, digits for numbers. Warm and calm. Confident without shouting. Read every line aloud as if to someone on their worst day; if it would sting, rewrite it.

## The concept: made to measure

A tailor does not ask your body to fit the suit. Daysize does not ask you to fit the plan. The flyer borrows the visual language of a **tailor's workshop**:

- a **measuring tape** whose markings are the hours of a day (06:00 to 22:00);
- **garment size tags** that read **1**, **3** and **5** instead of S, M and L;
- **sewing-pattern paper**: a faint grid, dotted cut lines, small scissor marks, notches;
- **tailor's chalk** strokes for hand-made emphasis;
- **a single visible stitch line** (dashed thread) that leads the eye through the page.

This must feel crafted and physical: paper, thread, chalk, cloth tags. It must not look like a tech app ad. No phone mock-up on the front as the hero; the product appears small, near the call to action.

## Brand system

### The wordmark

- **"Daysize" is set in the RobaRegular font**, and only the brand name uses it. Do not use RobaRegular for any other text.
- The founder supplies the font file. If you cannot use it, **leave the wordmark as a clearly marked placeholder box** labelled "Daysize in RobaRegular". Do **not** imitate it with another font.
- Write it as one word with a capital D: **Daysize**. Never "DaySize", "Day Size" or "DAYSIZE" in running text.
- Lock-up: the wordmark, with "by OwnYourSystem" below it in small caps of the body font, letter-spaced 8%, at 22% of the wordmark's height.
- Clear space around the wordmark: at least the height of its "D" on every side.

### Colour (new palette, not the app's plum)

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paper | Pattern Paper | `#F3ECDD` | Main background, like tailor's pattern paper |
| Ink | Workshop Ink | `#1F2A30` | Text, wordmark, line drawing |
| Tape | Tape Yellow | `#E9B949` | The measuring tape, the "3" tag |
| Thread | Thread Coral | `#D9573B` | The stitch line and one call-to-action shape only |
| Chalk | Chalk Blue | `#7FA6C9` | Chalk strokes, the grid, the "1" tag |
| Cloth | Linen Green | `#9DB59A` | The "5" tag, small fills |

Rules: Workshop Ink for all text (contrast at least 4.5:1 on Pattern Paper). Thread Coral is the single loud colour, used at most twice. No gradients, no glow, no drop shadows except a soft 2 mm paper shadow under the tags.

### Type

- **Brand name:** RobaRegular (see above).
- **Headlines and body:** a clean, humanist sans with good figures, for example **General Sans**, **Satoshi** or **Inter**. Pick one and use only it.
- **Tape and tag numbers:** a mono, for example **JetBrains Mono** or **IBM Plex Mono**, for the hour marks on the tape and the small print on the tags.
- Sizes on A5: headline 34 to 40 pt, subhead 12 to 13 pt, body 9.5 to 10.5 pt, small print no smaller than 7.5 pt.

## Flyer layout (A5 portrait, top to bottom)

1. **Top band (top 12%):** the Daysize wordmark lock-up, top left. Top right, small: "Early access".
2. **The hero (next 45%):** a measuring tape enters from the left edge, curls once and lies across the page. Its marks read 06:00 ... 22:00. Hanging from the tape on three short threads are **three size tags**, each with a punched hole and a string:
   - Tag **1** (Chalk Blue): "Rough day"
   - Tag **3** (Tape Yellow): "Normal day"
   - Tag **5** (Linen Green): "Good day"
   The tags are slightly rotated (-6°, 2°, 5°) like real tags, drawn flat with a fine ink outline.
3. **Headline**, set over the hero or just below it: **"Your day comes in three sizes."**
   Subhead: **"Daysize is a daily planner that gets smaller when your day does. On a rough day it asks for 1 thing. Every day counts."**
4. **The fitting notes (next 25%):** four short blocks laid out like the notes on a sewing pattern, each with a small line icon (needle, chalk, thimble, padlock), separated by dotted cut lines:
   - **One next step.** "One thing to do now, and why. It knows when your day ends."
   - **A minimum day.** "Up to 4 small things that count when nothing else moves."
   - **Quiet by design.** "2 reminders a day at most. Never after your day ends."
   - **Yours.** "Your journal and check-ins stay on your phone."
5. **The call to action (bottom 18%):** a coral stitched patch (Thread Coral outline, dashed stitch) holding: **"Try it on your phone"**, `[link]` and a `[QR code]` square. Beside it, small, a simple line drawing of a phone showing one card: "Do this now".
   Footer line, small: "Daysize by OwnYourSystem. Early access: installs from the browser, not in app stores yet. [launch date]"

The coral stitch line starts at the tape, runs down the left margin past the four notes and ends at the call-to-action patch, so the eye follows the thread.

## Illustration brief

- Style: flat ink line drawing (0.5 pt to 0.75 pt) with flat colour fills, slightly imperfect like hand-drawn work, on a faint pattern grid (Chalk Blue at 15% opacity, 5 mm squares).
- No people, no faces, no hands. The objects tell the story.
- Texture: a very light paper grain on the background (3 to 5% noise); none on the text.
- The tape's hour marks must be readable and correct (06:00, 08:00, 10:00 ... 22:00).

## Digital crops

- **Square (1080 × 1080):** the tape across the top third, the three tags centre, the headline below, the wordmark bottom left, `[link]` bottom right. Drop the four notes.
- **Story (1080 × 1920):** the tape runs vertically down the left side with hours top to bottom; the three tags hang to the right at 08:00, 12:00 and 18:00; the headline in the middle; the call-to-action patch at the bottom, above the safe zone (keep 250 px clear at top and bottom).

## Accessibility and print

- Text contrast at least 4.5:1. Never put text on Tape Yellow without Workshop Ink.
- The size tags carry a number and a word, never colour alone.
- Print: CMYK, 300 dpi, 3 mm bleed, 5 mm safe zone. Give the CMYK values for the 6 colours.

## Before you answer

Check every line against the hard rules and the product facts. If a sentence claims more than the facts give, cut it or rewrite it. Then give the deliverables in order: the flyer copy and layout, the illustration brief, the two crops, the claims list.
