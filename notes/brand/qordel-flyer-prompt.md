# Prompt: Qordel marketing flyer

Written 2026-10-21 for the live build 6f03082. Replaces `daysize-flyer-prompt.md` (old name; its
1, 3, 5 day sizes no longer exist). Copy everything below the line into the other AI or give it to a
designer.

---

## Your role

You are an art director and copywriter for a small, careful brand. You make print and social pieces
that people keep. Your work is calm, crafted and clear. You never use stock photos, clichés or hype.

## The brief in one line

Design a flyer that introduces **Qordel**, a daily planner that gives you one step at a time, in the
right order, and asks for nothing on the days you have nothing to give, using a "stepping stones"
concept that looks like nothing else in the productivity market.

## What to produce

1. **The flyer**: A5 portrait (148 × 210 mm), one side, print ready with 3 mm bleed. Final copy for
   every block, a layout description precise enough to build it, and the illustration brief.
2. **Two digital crops** of the same design: a square post (1080 × 1080 px) and a phone story
   (1080 × 1920 px). Re-arranged, not shrunk.
3. **A claims list**: every factual claim on the flyer, marked "true today" or "placeholder".
   Nothing goes on the flyer that is not on this list.

## The product (use only these facts; all true today unless marked)

- **Name:** Qordel. Made by **OwnYourSystem**. Tagline: **One step. In the right order.**
- **What it is:** a daily planner on your phone that shows one step at a time, with the reason in
  plain words.
- **Your energy decides the size of the day.** You set it in one tap (very low, low, good, full). If
  you do not, it stays at good.
- **On a very low day, or near the end of your day, it asks for nothing.** It says "Nothing is needed
  now" and shows your own easy things (an audiobook, a short walk). One of them counts the day.
- **No deadlines.** Steps go in their order. A goal can have one delivery day, shown as a calm pace
  ("about 2 a day"). If the day passes: "Nothing is lost. Pick a new day."
- **It learns how you work, and always asks first.** It might notice that you finish most things in the
  morning, and ask whether to keep that. You decide.
- **AI can suggest an order** for a goal's steps. You use it or keep yours.
- **Talk to it in plain words**, typed or spoken: "my energy is low", "I'm done with the report".
  `[A personal AI tutor answers free questions: use only once the founder confirms it is switched on]`
- **No typing needed:** pick an example goal with a tap, speak into any box, and have any card read aloud.
- **Quiet:** at most **2** reminders a day, never after your day ends.
- **Yours:** your board lives on your phone. Sign-in is optional. Your journal, weekly check-in and
  photo stay on your phone and are never shown to the AI.
- **Status:** early access. It runs in the phone's browser and installs to the home screen. It is
  **not** in the App Store or Google Play yet. No price yet.

## Hard rules

1. **Use only the facts above.** No invented user numbers, ratings, reviews, quotes, awards, prices or
   "as seen in". Use visible placeholders for what the founder fills: `[link]`, `[QR code]`, `[launch date]`.
2. **Not a health product.** It is calm on hard days; it does not treat, cure or diagnose anything. No
   claims about anxiety, stress, mental health or wellbeing results.
3. **AI is described by what it does,** never as a label ("AI-powered", "smart"). It suggests and
   learns; the person decides.
4. **No em dashes** anywhere in the copy. Use periods, commas, colons.
5. **Words to avoid:** revolutionary, game-changer, hack, hustle, crush it, 10x, streak, guaranteed,
   unlock, supercharge, deadline, overdue, behind.
6. **Words that fit the brand:** one step, in the right order, your energy, nothing is needed now, your
   easy things, you decide, it asks first, yours, quiet.
7. **Voice:** plain English, short sentences, active voice, digits for numbers. Warm and calm. Read
   every line aloud as if to someone on their worst day; if it would sting, rewrite it.

## The concept: stepping stones

A river is crossed one stone at a time, in order. You never need to see the whole river, only the next
stone. And when you are tired, you can sit on the bank; the river waits.

- A calm river runs across the page, drawn in a few flat bands, not realistic water.
- **Stepping stones** cross it in a gentle curve, in order. Most are plain stone grey. **One stone, the
  next one, is filled with Moonlit Plum** and has a small tag beside it: "Next: buy the timber" (an
  example step, in the app's own words).
- **The stones already crossed** behind it carry a small tick each.
- **On the near bank, a simple bench** with a small sign: "Nothing is needed now." This is the rest day,
  shown as a good place to be, not as falling behind.
- **On the far bank, a small flag** for a goal, with a soft label: "Garden shed. About 2 a day." No
  dates, no clocks.

It must feel crafted and physical: paper, ink line, flat colour. It must not look like a tech app ad.
The phone appears small, near the call to action, never as the hero.

## Brand system

### The wordmark

- **QORDEL in capitals, Schibsted Grotesk SemiBold, letter-spacing -0.02em.** Only the name uses this
  lock-up. In running text write "Qordel".
- Lock-up: the wordmark, with "by OwnYourSystem" below it in the body font, regular weight, at 38% of
  the wordmark's height.
- Clear space: at least the height of the "Q" on every side.
- The app icon: QORDEL in white on Moonlit Plum, rounded square.

### Colour

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ground | Morning Grey | `#F2F2F7` | Main background |
| Ink | Ink | `#1C1C1E` | Text, wordmark, line drawing |
| Accent | Moonlit Plum | `#2C1332` | The next stone, the call to action, the icon. The only strong colour. |
| Soft | Lilac | `#D8C2E0` | The flag, small highlights |
| Stone | Stone | `#C7C4CC` | The stepping stones, the bench |
| Water | River | `#DCE4EC` | The river bands |

Rules: Ink for all text (at least 4.5:1 on Morning Grey). Moonlit Plum used in at most 3 places. No
gradients, no glow, no drop shadows.

### Type

- **Headlines and body:** Schibsted Grotesk (SemiBold for headlines, Regular for body).
- **Small labels and numbers:** DM Mono, for the stone tag and the "about 2 a day" label.
- Sizes on A5: headline 32 to 38 pt, subhead 12 to 13 pt, body 9.5 to 10.5 pt, nothing under 7.5 pt.

## Flyer layout (A5 portrait, top to bottom)

1. **Top band (12%):** the QORDEL lock-up, top left. Top right, small: "Early access".
2. **The hero (45%):** the river, the stones, the plum next stone with its tag, the bench on the near
   bank, the flag on the far bank.
3. **Headline:** **"One step. In the right order."**
   Subhead: **"Qordel shows you the next step and why. On the days you have nothing to give, it asks
   for nothing."**
4. **Four short notes (25%)**, each with a small line icon, separated by thin rules:
   - **Your energy decides.** "Tell it how you are. The day fits that."
   - **No deadlines.** "Steps in order, at a calm pace. Nothing is lost."
   - **It learns, and asks first.** "It notices how you work. You decide what it keeps."
   - **No typing needed.** "Tap an example, speak, or have it read aloud."
5. **Call to action (18%):** a plum rounded panel with **"Try it on your phone"**, `[link]` and a
   `[QR code]`. Beside it, small, a line drawing of a phone showing one card: "Do this now".
   Footer, small: "Qordel by OwnYourSystem. Early access: installs from the browser, not in app stores
   yet. [launch date]"

## Illustration brief

- Flat ink line (0.5 to 0.75 pt) with flat colour fills, slightly hand-drawn, on Morning Grey.
- No people, no faces, no hands. The stones, bench and flag tell the story.
- A very light paper grain on the background (3 to 5% noise), none on text.
- The tag on the plum stone and the flag label must be readable at print size.

## Digital crops

- **Square (1080 × 1080):** the river and stones across the middle, the bench bottom left, the
  headline above, the wordmark top left, `[link]` bottom right. Drop the four notes.
- **Story (1080 × 1920):** the stones climb from the bottom to the top of the screen like a path, the
  plum stone in the middle third, the bench at the bottom, the headline at the top, the call to action
  above the bottom safe zone (keep 250 px clear at top and bottom).

## Accessibility and print

- Text contrast at least 4.5:1. Never put text on River or Stone without Ink.
- The next stone is marked by its tag and its fill, never by colour alone.
- Print: CMYK, 300 dpi, 3 mm bleed, 5 mm safe zone. Give the CMYK values for the 6 colours.

## Before you answer

Check every line against the hard rules and the product facts. If a sentence claims more than the facts
give, cut it or rewrite it. Then give the deliverables in order: the flyer copy and layout, the
illustration brief, the two crops, the claims list.
