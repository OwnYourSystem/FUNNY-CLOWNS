# User sessions with people who live with anxiety or stress

Written 2026-10-21 for the gap "proof that it helps". Qordel is designed to add no pressure. Only
people who live with anxiety or stress can tell us whether it actually feels calm. This is a plan
to run 5 to 8 sessions. It is a usability study, not a clinical trial and not therapy.

## The questions

1. Does any screen, word or notification raise stress? Which, and why?
2. Does the person understand why Qordel shows the step it shows?
3. Does "Nothing is needed now" feel like permission, or like a judgement?
4. Is it easy to use without typing (voice, taps, read aloud)?
5. Does learning ("Something I noticed") feel helpful or watched?

## Who

- 5 to 8 adults who say they live with anxiety or ongoing stress. No diagnosis is asked for or
  recorded. Mix: some who do not like typing, at least 1 who uses a screen reader or large text,
  at least 2 who have stopped using planners because they felt guilty.
- Not now: anyone in an acute crisis. If the screener suggests that, thank them and give the
  support numbers below.
- Thank-you payment for the time (for example a voucher for 45 minutes).

## Consent and safety

- Plain-language consent before the session: what we do, 45 minutes, they can stop at any moment
  without a reason, what is recorded, how long it is kept, how to withdraw later.
- Record the screen and voice only with a separate yes. Notes use a code, never a name.
- Distress protocol: if a participant becomes upset, stop the task, ask if they want a pause or
  to end, do not coach or counsel. Have local support numbers ready (country helplines and the
  emergency number) and offer them calmly. Note it and do not count it as a failed task.
- Use the participant's own phone where possible, on the preview link, with a fresh board.

## Session plan (45 minutes)

| Minutes | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 5 | Welcome, consent, "there are no wrong answers; we test the app, not you" | |
| 5 to 10 | Short warm-up: how do they plan a day today, what makes a planner stressful | Their words, not ours |
| 10 to 35 | Tasks below, thinking aloud | Do not help unless they are stuck for 2 minutes |
| 35 to 42 | Questions after the tasks | |
| 42 to 45 | Thank you, payment, the support numbers sheet | |

## Tasks

1. Open Qordel for the first time and add one goal, without typing if they prefer.
2. Tell Qordel how your energy is today.
3. Look at Now. "What is it asking you to do, and why?"
4. Skip the step. Then bring it back.
5. Set your energy to Very low. "How does this screen make you feel?"
6. A delivery day has passed (seeded). "What would you do here?"
7. A "Something I noticed" card (seeded). Keep it or Not now, and why.
8. Ask the assistant something about your day, by voice. Use the speaker to hear the answer.

After each task ask one question: **"How calm did that feel, from 1 (very tense) to 5 (very calm)?"**

## Questions after the tasks

- Was there a moment you felt pushed, judged or behind? Where?
- Which words would you change?
- Would you open this on a bad day? What would stop you?
- Did the app feel like it was watching you? Was asking first enough?
- Compared with a to-do list you know, is this calmer, the same, or worse?

## What to measure

- The 1 to 5 calm rating per task (median and the lowest score per task).
- Task success without help (yes / with a hint / no).
- Every word or screen that made someone tense, with a quote.
- Whether they say they would use it on a bad day.

## Afterwards

Write up within 2 days: a table of tasks with calm ratings and success, the top 5 problems with
quotes, and a proposed change for each. Delete recordings after the write-up unless the
participant agreed to longer keeping.
