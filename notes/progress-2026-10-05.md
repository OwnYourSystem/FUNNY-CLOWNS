## Known limits

- Push: the 07:30 morning brief arrived on the owner's device on 2026-10-06, so the server, the schedule and the device side work together. Not yet confirmed: the 15:00 check, iPhone, other devices and timing accuracy over many days.
- The assistant understands set phrases and asks when unsure. It is not a free chat. It has no "tomorrow" list, so "move this to tomorrow" does not work.
- Everything is in English.
- No undo for assistant changes, no "delete my data" on the server, no monitoring of the push job. None of these is agreed yet.
