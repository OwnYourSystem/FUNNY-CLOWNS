# The merged values, compared with the board

Written 2026-10-02. Analysis only. No app change has been made or decided.
Values come from `merged-analysis.md` (section 3). Board facts come from
reading `source/delivery-board.html`. I did not run the app for this.

## The values

V1 Find capacity first. V2 Name the barrier. V3 Set a floor. V4 Pick one action, smallest version. V5 Make starting cheap. V6 Stay in the stretch zone and recover on purpose. V7 Failure is data, not a verdict. V8 Measure the person, not only output. V9 Safety limit: no clinical claims, and a way out when the person is not okay.

## Where the board pulls against the values

1. **It pushes harder as you fall behind.** The urgency curve (overdue = 100) is the opposite of "work slightly below capacity on a low day". With no capacity input, a bad week makes the verdict louder.
2. **The board decides for you.** The verdict is a directive. The values aim to make the person able to read their own state. Skip exists, but the reason for a skip is not captured.
3. **The scoreboard is output only.** R1 says a person can be more productive while sleep and anxiety get worse. The board cannot see that.
4. **The tutor refuses off-topic input.** That is a safe default for scope. It is the wrong reply for "I can't cope".

## Where the board is ahead of the notes

- The "why now" sentence is concrete and short. The notes describe the idea; the board already does it.
- Context rules (time, place, commute, wind-down) are stronger than anything in either note.
- "The one thing that matters most" gives meaning and autonomy a place (R1 #1, "doesn't want to").
- Repeating habits with streaks cover habit formation.
- Quiet tone with no hype matches the "no moral verdict" value.

## Smallest steps that would close the biggest gaps

Not started. Listed by value per effort.

1. **Capacity tap (V1, V6).** One 0 to 10 tap at the morning brief. Use R1's bands: 0 to 3 protect, 4 to 6 normal, 7 to 8 challenge, 9 to 10 do not overreach. Feed the band into the verdict, so a low day shrinks the dose and drops the overdue multiplier.
2. **Miss note (V2, V7).** When a planned item is not done by evening, one tap: can't, afraid, don't know how, don't want to. Store it with expected and done.
3. **Weekly 5 (V8).** Energy, mood, anxiety, functioning, meaningful action, 1 to 5 each, shown beside Overall. Flag when output rises while the other four fall.
4. **Distress reply (V9).** One fixed line in the tutor rules for "not okay" input: say it cannot help with that, suggest a professional, make no clinical claim.
5. **Floor line (V3).** A "minimum day" strip: three anchors that count even when nothing else moves.

My read: do 1 and 4 first. 1 changes the verdict itself. 4 is one rule and closes a real gap. Waiting on your call.
