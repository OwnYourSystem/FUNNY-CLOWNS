# Terms

This is a draft. A legal professional must review it before it is published.
It describes only what Qordel does today, taken from the code on 2026-10-25.

## What Qordel is

Qordel is a day planner. It suggests one next step at a time, sized to your
energy and your calendar. You decide what to accept. The board makes no change
without your own action, and a change can be undone.

## Using Qordel

- Qordel is for your own planning. Use it lawfully.
- Your board, journal and check-ins live on your device. Keep your own backups;
  clearing your browser clears them.
- An account and reminders are optional. You can use the board without either.

## The AI

The "Suggest an order" feature uses Anthropic to propose an order for the steps
of one goal. It is a suggestion. Nothing changes until you accept it, and you
can undo it. A conversational tutor is built but switched off today.

The AI gives care and attention, not medical or mental-health advice. It is not
a person. Do not rely on it for decisions that need a professional.

## Reminders

If you turn on reminders, Qordel sends at most two a day, and never after your
day has ended. A late reminder is dropped, not sent late. You can turn
reminders off at any time.

## What we do not promise

Qordel is provided as it is. We do not promise it will be free of faults or
always available. The AI and the reminders depend on outside services that can
fail; the board still works without them.
